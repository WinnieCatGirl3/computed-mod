    Ideas & Roadmap¶

    This page collects what is planned for Winnie's Computed — and it is a thread you can write in. Everything here starts as a community idea.

    Have an idea?

    Open a post in the Ideas discussion thread on GitHub. Reply to existing posts to vote with a 👍, or start your own — a short title and one or two sentences are enough. The best ideas get picked up here.

    Open ideas¶

    Ideas the community and the maintainer still want to build. Click through to the thread if you want to comment on one.

    Speaker and audio¶

    • Custom instrument samples — drop your own .ogg files into the speaker folder to use them as instruments and waveforms.

    Widgets and display¶

    • Chart widget — plot values over time as a curve (progress bar, checkbox, and text input already exist).
    • Image widget — show a texture or item icon on the monitor.
    • Right-click widget actions — a second action column for widgets (for example a context menu).

    Computer and world¶

    • Radar / sensor endpoint — query entities, light level, weather, or blocks in a radius (scan(radius) returns a table).
    • Energy endpoint — read and extract FE/EU (Create / Forge Energy integration).

    Integrations¶

    • Create screens as monitors — bind Create's display blocks as a monitor surface (kinetic speed and stress already work through the Create Kinetic node).

    Recently shipped¶

    A short look at what the thread already produced:

    • Speaker: Play Sequence (timed instrument:note:seconds melodies without clock nodes) and a disc playlist — queue up to 8 discs with right-click, play advances through them, loop cycles the whole queue.
    • Autostart disk — right-click a written Program Disk to flag it; the computer loads and runs the program at startup or as soon as the flagged disk is inserted (through the regular validation, an existing program always wins).
    • has_display() — computed:monitor/has_display and the Display Present node answer whether a wall monitor is attached at a face or a player carries a paired portable monitor.
    • Redstone endpoints — computed:redstone reads input/comparator levels and writes output at the computer's faces (Redstone Input, Comparator Read, Redstone Emitter nodes).
    • Speaker: Play Sweep (frequency glide), attack/decay envelopes on Play Wave, and disc loop for endless playback.
    • Aeronautics: vertical speed and altitude nodes, plus the Threshold Trigger node for alarms (altitude below X, overspeed, climb warnings).
    • Glasses HUD: graph widgets on your glasses, a movable widget region in the K designer, and a reworked glasses model with its own recipe.
    • Undo/redo and auto-save in the editor, the Lua live preview, and a demo disk in the creative tab.
    • Portable monitor: right-click a channel tab to remove it, and tabs show computer names.
    • Computer-to-computer messaging (computed:net), the Aeronautics telemetry nodes, and the Create Kinetic sensor.

    Raw backlog

    The maintainer's working list lives in IDEAS.md in the repository — the thread is the place to add to it.
